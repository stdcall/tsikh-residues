# Multidimensional Residues and Their Applications

A. K. Tsikh, *Multidimensional Residues and Their Applications*,
Translations of Mathematical Monographs 103. American Mathematical Society,
Providence, Rhode Island, 1992. ISBN 0-8218-4560-8. Translated from the
Russian edition (Nauka, Novosibirsk, 1988) by E. J. F. Primrose; translation
edited by Sergei Gelfand.

The book develops the theory of multidimensional residues and applies it.
Chapter I recalls singular and smooth homology, semianalytic chains,
differential forms and currents, and complex analytic sets and ideals.
Chapter II treats residues of holomorphic mappings f: Cⁿ → Cⁿ: the local
(Grothendieck) residue, its transformation formula and local duality, its
expression through the trace, the theorem on the total sum of residues on a
compact manifold, global residues of polynomial mappings, the
representability of integrals by residues, and residues depending on a
parameter. Chapter III treats mappings f: Cⁿ → Cᵖ: residual currents and
principal values (the Coleff–Herrera theory), normal intersections, the
main theorem on residual currents and its proof, the Leray residue formula
and fibered residues. Chapter IV applies residues to Weil's integral
formula, the local Nullstellensatz and the Weierstrass preparation and
division theorems, weakly holomorphic functions on complete intersections,
the logarithmic residue and multiplicities of zeros, Bézout's theorem, and
ideals in a polynomial ring (the theorems of Macaulay and Noether).
Chapter V applies them to the singularities of the Hadamard composition of
double series, to the quantification error of a two-dimensional numerical
recursive filter, and to a class of integrals over R².

The Typst edition keeps the pagination of the front matter; the page breaks
of the chapters differ from those of the 1992 edition. It has a linked
contents, bibliography (with DOIs), list of notations and subject index.
Errors of the 1992 edition are corrected; editorial notes explain the few
corrections a reader needs explained, and their literature is listed as
Editorial References after the bibliography. Each release includes the
reader's PDF and a separate list of corrections. `checks/` holds exact
computations in SageMath and formal steps in Lean that support the
corrections (see [checks/README.md](checks/README.md)). See
